This page showcases our current work and offers a glimpse into the future we are building.

FEATURED PROJECT

The Saved

The Saved is an original teenage action animation currently in development by Chazon Studios.

Blending exciting action, compelling characters, emotional storytelling, and biblical themes, the project explores faith, courage, identity, sacrifice, and redemption.

Rather than simply entertaining, The Saved is being created to encourage viewers to reflect on God, grow in faith, and discover hope through a meaningful cinematic experience.

Status: Currently In Development
